Who We Are

Four Decades of Signage
Craftsmanship in Sri Lanka

Kekulawala Advertising is a family-grown advertising and signage workshop based in Kurunegala, serving businesses across the island since 1982. What began as a small sign-writing studio is today a full-service production house powered by digital printing, laser fabrication and a passionate team of craftspeople.

  • Complete in-house workflow

    Design, printing, laser cutting, fabrication and installation — no middlemen.

  • Weather-grade materials

    UV-stable inks, marine-grade vinyl and durable metalwork built for the tropics.

  • Island-wide delivery & fitting

    From boutique storefronts in Colombo to roadside hoardings in the provinces.

How We Work

From Idea to Installation

A simple, transparent process refined over four decades.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We discuss your site, audience and budget, then survey the location for measurements and visibility.

  2. 02

    Design & Proof

    Our designers craft mock-ups with material specs, so you approve exactly what will be produced.

  3. 03

    Production

    Digital printing, laser cutting and assembly happen in our Kurunegala workshop with strict QC.

  4. 04

    Delivery & Install

    Our fitting team transports and installs island-wide, then hands over with aftercare guidance.
