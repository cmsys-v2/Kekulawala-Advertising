ABOUT KEKULAWALA

The North Western Province's most trusted advertising workshop

Founded in 1982, Kekulawala Advertising grew from a humble sign-writing studio into a fully-equipped production house — with in-house wide-format digital printing, CNC routing, laser cutting & engraving, steel fabrication and a dedicated installation fleet.

Banks, hotels, super-markets, hospitals, schools and thousands of small businesses trust one name when their brand needs to be seen — from Kurunegala to every corner of the island.

  • Everything under one roof — design, print, fabricate & install
  • Island-wide delivery & professional installation crews
  • Premium materials with workmanship warranty
  • Fast turnarounds — most signs ready in 3–7 working days
